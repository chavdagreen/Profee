Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Profee ("we", "our", or "us") is a practice management application designed exclusively for Chartered Accountants and tax professionals in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application at profee.in.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you sign in with Google.
  • Client Data: Names, PAN numbers, contact details, and case information you enter about your clients. This data is stored securely and is only accessible to you.
  • Google Calendar Data: When you choose to connect Google Calendar, we create calendar events for your litigation hearings. We do not read, modify, or delete any existing calendar events.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Profee practice management features.
  • To sync your hearing dates and deadlines to your Google Calendar (only when you explicitly connect and initiate sync).
  • To authenticate your identity securely via Firebase Authentication.

3. Google Calendar Integration

Profee requests access to your Google Calendar solely to create hearing and deadline events on your behalf. Specifically:

  • We use the calendar.events scope to create new calendar events for your tax litigation hearings.
  • We do not read, access, share, or delete any existing events on your calendar.
  • We do not share your calendar data with any third party.
  • You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from within the app.
  • Revoking access via Google Account Permissions will immediately stop all calendar access.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), secured by Firebase Authentication. Only you can access your own data.
  • Client passwords stored in Profee are for your reference only and are not transmitted to any third party.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or your clients' data with any third party.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you maintain an active account. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and database storage (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Calendar API — For optional hearing sync (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Vercel — Application hosting (Vercel Privacy Policy)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
